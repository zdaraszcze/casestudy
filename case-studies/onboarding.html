Problem Statement

Current State

Process Overview

The current onboarding process spans 9 phases and 148 discrete steps, involving Client Advisors, Onboarding Specialists, Due Diligence Officers, and Client Operations teams across more than 20 systems. These systems operate without a shared data layer or orchestration logic. Client data entered in one platform does not propagate to others. Handoffs between phases are managed by email and shared drives rather than workflow automation.

The process was not designed end-to-end. It evolved incrementally as regulatory requirements and system capabilities were added over time. Manual effort now bridges the gaps between systems. Quality at each phase depends on individual practitioner discipline. The platform enforces nothing.

Design Challenge

How might the onboarding process be redesigned so that data flows automatically between phases, compliance quality is enforced at point of entry rather than corrected at point of review, and each actor operates from a single context rather than navigating 20+ disconnected systems?

Design Mandate

Straight-through processing for standard cases. AI-augmented quality assurance for EDD-required cases. Automated orchestration of document generation, signing coordination, and product opening.

In Scope
  • Prospect creation and data capture (Phases 2–3)
  • PEP pre-check integration (Phase 4)
  • Enhanced Due Diligence workflow (Phase 5)
  • Product selection and configuration (Phase 6)
  • Document preparation and formalities (Phase 7)
  • Video identification and signing coordination (Phase 8)
  • Business Relationship and product opening (Phase 9)
Out of Scope: v1
  • Lead generation optimisation (Phase 1: separate initiative)
  • Post-onboarding portfolio management
  • Core Banking system replacement
  • Legal Archive platform migration
  • Mobile-native application
  • Discretionary mandate or trade execution flows

Key Findings

01

Data Entered Once, Used Nowhere

Data captured in the Onboarding Platform does not write back to the CA Portal or populate the document generation tool. Correspondence instructions entered during data capture never flow downstream, causing generated documents to contain blank or incorrect fields. Client Advisors re-enter the same data in three to five separate systems per case.

"I fill in the same client address in the onboarding form, then again in the document tool, then again when we need to generate a letter. There's no logic to it."Client Advisor
02

EDD Quality is a Front-Office Problem Solved in the Back Office

The Enhanced Due Diligence phase achieves a first-time-right rate of approximately 20%, based on participant recall corroborated by observed case queues. The primary cause is insufficient source-of-wealth narrative quality at submission, not regulatory threshold disagreement. Due Diligence Officers receive Word-template-based files with manually copied data and apply judgment that could be better informed by structured inputs. The 14-step sequential approval chain amplifies the cost of each iteration.

Baseline figure of ~20% requires validation against institutional case management data before targets are confirmed.

"The CA submits what they think is enough. I can see within 30 seconds it will need to come back. But I have to process it formally before I can return it."Due Diligence Officer
03

Formalities Preparation Consumes Disproportionate Specialist Time

The Formalities phase involves 28 discrete manual sub-steps for document preparation. The workflow spans the Onboarding Platform, a document generation tool accessed via email, Adobe Acrobat for merging and flattening, DocBasket for QR code generation, and a shared drive for version management. No audit trail exists on the shared drive. Front office modifications to generated documents are undetectable after the fact. Onboarding Specialists estimate approximately 60% of their Phase 7 time goes on this mechanical work, not on checking whether cases are complete.

"I spend more time creating folder structures and flattening PDFs than I do checking whether a case is actually complete."Client Onboarding Specialist
04

The Signing and Opening Experience Frustrates Prospects at the Finish Line

Prospects identified via original documents cannot switch to electronic signing without a video identification call, and no online identification alternative exists. This creates a hard scheduling dependency that blocks same-day or asynchronous completion for any prospect identified by paper documents. Accounts are blocked for up to 3 days post-opening pending an overnight archive verification batch process, meaning prospects who complete signing and receive confirmation cannot access their account immediately.

"By the time we reach signing, the client has been waiting weeks. Then we tell them the account needs another 3 days to activate. The goodwill from completing the process evaporates."Client Advisor

TO-BE Journey Map

The redesigned journey targets end-to-end cycle time below 5 days for standard cases and below 14 days for EDD-required cases. Nine interventions cover every phase of the journey. EDD first-time-right directional target: 70% or above, up from approximately 20% baseline. All targets are subject to technical validation and baseline measurement.

Intervention 1: Prospect Deduplication Real-time duplicate detection and automated Type I to Type II promotion eliminates manual conversion and downstream data mismatches. MDM integration, no ML dependency.
Intervention 2: Document AI + Writeback OCR auto-populates ONB fields from uploaded documents. Bidirectional writeback to CA Portal ensures downstream document generation is pre-filled. Addresses Phase 3 core pain.
Intervention 3: Integrated PEP Screening PEP and sanctions check embedded in ONB Platform via API. Eliminates standalone Excel step. Risk flags written to case automatically. Addresses Phase 4 core pain.
Intervention 4: EDD Gen AI (SOW / SIAP) Gen AI assesses source-of-wealth narrative and SIAP risk at point of document upload. Real-time guidance reduces iteration rate. Target: 20% to 70% first-time-right. Regulatory approval required.

Intervention 6: Automated Product Opening Case approval triggers Core Banking account opening automatically. PICH and CDOC tagging automated. Resolution of 3-day blocking is a Core Banking programme dependency, outside UX scope.

Design Principles

01

Data Enters Once

Client data collected at any point in the journey is written to a shared case record accessible to all downstream systems. No actor re-enters information already captured. Platform integrations enforce this, and no bypass is available.

Derived from: Finding 01: Data Entered Once, Used Nowhere. Corroborated across all 14 CA diary study participants.

02

Quality Enforced at Entry, Not Corrected at Review

Compliance quality problems are diagnosed at the point of data capture, not discovered after submission. AI-assisted guidance surfaces gaps in source-of-wealth narratives, document completeness, and field validity before a case moves to review. The cost of an EDD iteration is eliminated, not managed.

Derived from: Finding 02: EDD Quality is a Front-Office Problem Solved in the Back Office. EDD first-time-right baseline ~20% (recall-based).

03

Status is Always Visible

Every actor in the onboarding process (Client Advisor, Onboarding Specialist, Due Diligence Officer, and prospect) has real-time visibility of where the case stands, what is pending, and who owns the next action. No actor learns of a problem by email. No prospect infers status from silence.

Derived from: Finding 04: Signing and Opening Experience. Corroborated across all 10 HNW prospect participants.

04

Complexity is the Platform's Responsibility

Joint accounts, complex entity structures, multi-jurisdiction domiciles, and cross-border product rules are the platform's responsibility, not configuration tasks delegated to the advisor. Structural complexity should not change the cognitive load of working a case. A Client Advisor facing a complex onboarding and a simple one should have the same experience.

Derived from: Finding 03: Formalities Preparation. 28-step manual process for all case types regardless of complexity.

05

Automation Redirects Attention, Not Headcount

Automating routine tasks in Formalities and Product Opening frees Onboarding Specialist and Client Operations capacity for exception handling, quality review, and client-facing work. Efficiency from automation should translate into better service, not into headcount reduction.

Derived from: Finding 03. Onboarding Specialists estimate ~60% of Phase 7 time is spent on low-value mechanical tasks.

1
AI Risk Score (87/100): Score displayed with colour band (green: 70–100, amber: 40–69, red: 0–39). Clicking the score expands a full breakdown of contributing factors. Score is never the only input: CO always has full access to underlying evidence. Explainability is a first-class feature, not a tooltip.
2
Adverse media flag: The ⚠ symbol indicates the AI has flagged something but assessed it as non-blocking. This is a deliberate design decision: surface all AI findings, not just negatives. 3
Approve action: "Approve" button triggers a confirmation modal listing: client name, risk score, key flags, CO's name, and timestamp. 4
RFI flow: "Raise RFI" opens an inline panel, not a new page, with pre-populated request templates (e.g., "Additional SOW evidence required for [field]"). 5
Audit trail: Every view, action, and note on this screen is timestamped and attributed. The audit log is immutable and exportable as a regulator-ready PDF. Edge case: If CO is logged out mid-review due to session timeout, all unsaved notes are preserved in a draft state and restored on next login.

Regulatory Dependencies

REG
EDD Gen AI: Phase 5

Use of generative AI in EDD risk classification requires: (a) Compliance and Legal sign-off; (b) model governance documentation; (c) validation methodology demonstrating consistent, non-discriminatory outputs; (d) potential regulatory notification under applicable frameworks. Proposed owner: Chief Compliance Officer. This intervention is proposed as a design direction; implementation is conditional on regulatory approval.

REG
4-Eye Replacement: Phase 7

Replacement of the 4-eye compliance control with AI-assisted document verification requires Compliance and Legal review and may require regulatory notification. The 4-eye principle has standing under multiple applicable frameworks. The proposed AI exception queue must be formally assessed against internal control requirements before implementation. Proposed owner: Compliance and Legal.

REG
Online Identification Alternative: Phase 8

Legal review required to confirm the eligibility criteria and acceptable methods for an online identification alternative for clients currently identified by original documents. Proposed owner: Legal.

Technical Dependencies

TECH
Core Banking API: Phase 9

Automated product opening and the resolution of the 3-day account blocking constraint require Core Banking development investment and a parallel programme outside the scope of this UX engagement. The UX design delivers the triggering interface; the Core Banking programme delivers the unblocking capability. Proposed owner: Technology Programme Management.

TECH
CA Portal Writeback API: Phase 3

Bidirectional data writeback between the Onboarding Platform and CA Portal requires API development between currently disconnected platforms. API ownership and feasibility to be confirmed with platform owners. Proposed owner: Platform Engineering.

TECH
PEP Screening Tool API: Phase 4

Integration of PEP and sanctions screening within the ONB Platform requires an API connection to the existing PEP Screening Tool. API availability and integration pattern to be confirmed with the PEP Screening Tool vendor or owner team.

TECH
PICH Trigger Conditions: Phase 9

Automated PICH GF115 triggering from recorded risk flags requires verification of trigger conditions with the relevant programme manager. Conditions should be mapped and confirmed before automation logic is built.

Data and Measurement

DATA
EDD Baseline Validation

The EDD first-time-right rate of approximately 20% is based on participant recall across DDO and CA interviews. This figure should be validated against institutional case management data (case volume, return rate, iteration count) before improvement targets are confirmed and presented to programme sponsors.

DATA
Improvement Target Rationale

All improvement targets in this document (EDD 70% first-time-right, formalities 28 steps to single workflow, cycle time 35–55 days to 5–14 days) are directional targets based on the journey map analysis. They require baseline measurement confirmation and, for the EDD AI target, a comparable implementation reference or bottom-up model accuracy assessment before being presented as programme commitments.

Organisational Considerations

ORG
Client Onboarding Specialist Role Impact: Phase 7

Automation of the 28-step formalities preparation process materially changes the Client Onboarding Specialist role. Approximately 60% of current task time in this phase consists of low-value mechanical work. A change management plan is required to address role redefinition, retraining, and team communication before this intervention is deployed. Proposed owner: HR and Operations.

ORG
Client Operations Team Impact: Phase 9

Automated product opening and CDOC tagging in Phase 9 reduces the manual processing workload of the Client Operations team. A workforce impact assessment should be conducted in parallel with technical scoping. Proposed owner: Operations Management.

ORG
AI Governance Framework

An AI governance framework applicable to the EDD Gen AI agent and document verification interventions has not been identified in this engagement. Before Phase 5 and Phase 7 AI interventions proceed, a determination is required on which institutional AI governance policy applies, who owns model validation, and what the approval pathway is.
