No case closes without a reason on record.

Result A visible deadline, records side by side, and a closure flow that can't be skipped

  1. Situation

    Banks must check the ownership records they hold on business clients against a public registry, and about a third don't match. Once flagged, a case has 90 days before it becomes a mandatory government report.

  2. Task

    At JPB I was brought in to design the tool compliance officers use to work these cases.

  3. Action

    Interviews and shadowed sessions with officers doing this by hand showed the real problem: the deadline was invisible, the fault could sit on either side, and closing a case needed a real trail, not a note written after the fact.

  4. Result

    A workspace built around those three things: a countdown that follows the officer everywhere, the two records shown side by side, and a decision flow that won't let a case close without a reason on record.

From hand-written JSON to a guided path builder.

Result One layout serving four roles, and errors caught before submit instead of after

  1. Situation

    GraphWalk's Workspace held everything in flat tiles built for a handful of items, now holding 80 or more link charts per person with no hierarchy. Creating a new relationship meant hand-writing JSON with raw database IDs, and errors only surfaced as a 400 after submitting.

  2. Task

    Fix both problems without separate screens per role and without touching the backend or API.

  3. Action

    Working from requirements and docs rather than fresh interviews, I tagged every claim as fact or assumption so nothing got silently treated as settled. Then one answer for both problems: role-based views, and a guided path builder with live validation and a preview.

  4. Result

    Still a specification. The open call on how much oversight new paths need before they save is pending.

One live view of the whole base station.

Result Target: about 20% faster commissioning and fewer truck rolls

  1. Situation

    A Single-RAN base station runs three mobile generations on shared hardware, but engineers were diagnosing it through flat text logs split by technology. Faults got misread and trucks rolled on guesses.

  2. Task

    Lead UX design and research on a 6-month redesign of the diagnostics and maintenance console.

  3. Action

    One live topology view, one visual language across 20+ hardware types, and a staged commissioning wizard that catches errors as they happen.

  4. Result

    Design targets of about 20% faster commissioning and fewer avoidable truck rolls, with a validation plan attached at handoff.
